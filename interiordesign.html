What's Included

Every project starts with an in-home consultation. We see the light, the flow, and the pieces you love in person, take measurements, and talk through your style, budget, and timeline.

From there, you'll get:

  • A design direction with colors, textures, and finishes
  • A furniture layout measured to your room
  • A curated list of furniture, lighting, and decor
  • Optional sourcing, ordering, and delivery coordination
  • An install day where we style everything in place

How It Works

  1. Complete the intake. Share your rooms, style, budget range, and the days that work for your consultation.
  2. We meet at your home. We walk the space, take measurements, and talk through how you live and what you love.
  3. You receive your design and estimate. Layout, design direction, and a curated product list sized to your budget.
  4. We bring it to life. Purchase on your own, or let us source, coordinate delivery, and style everything on install day.
