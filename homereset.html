Why We Offer Home Resets

As a busy mom, I once had someone come every Monday to reset our home. Dishes, laundry, snacks for the week, and the playroom put back together. It changed how our whole week felt, and now we offer that same help to you.

Richelle, Owner

Home Resets are light upkeep, not cleaning. They pair beautifully with a monthly or twice monthly clean from our cleaning team.

Home Resets start at $260 per reset. Your final price depends on your home’s size, how cluttered it is, the tasks you need, and how many kids and pets are in the home.

Visits are Monday through Friday. Choose weekly or every other week to keep your home feeling reset, or book a one time reset when life gets busy.

What’s Included

You choose the tasks that matter most to your household. Here’s what a Home Reset can cover.

Kitchen

Kitchen Reset

Dishes washed and put away, counters wiped, and the sink cleared.

Laundry

Wash, Dry, Fold

Laundry washed, dried, folded, and put away. Bedding changes on request.

Food Prep

Snack and Meal Prep

Snacks portioned, produce washed and cut, and lunches prepped for the week.

Kids’ Spaces

Playroom and Bedrooms

Toys back in their bins, books on the shelf, and beds made.

Pets

Pet Touches

Dog bed covers and couch covers washed and put back on.

Week Ahead

Ready for Monday

Light vacuuming, drop zones and backpacks reset, and the house ready for the week.

Not included: mirrors, showers, toilets, or mopping. Those are part of our cleaning services, which you can book separately.

How Home Resets Work

  1. Submit the Home Reset intake. Tell us about your household and the help you’d like.
  2. We send your quote. It’s based on your home size, family, and task list.
  3. We set your schedule. Weekly, every other week, or a one time reset, Monday through Friday.
  4. Enjoy your week. Come home to a house that feels calm and ready.
