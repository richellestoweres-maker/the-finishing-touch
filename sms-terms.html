Program name

The Finishing Touch SMS Alerts.

Program description

By opting in, you agree to receive text messages from The Finishing Touch, a residential cleaning and home-services business. Messages include appointment reminders, scheduling and confirmation messages, and occasional promotional offers and seasonal updates.

How to opt in

You opt in by submitting your phone number and checking the consent box on our booking or intake forms at thefinishingtouch-tx.com.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your appointments and our promotions.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. The Finishing Touch does not charge for the messages, but your mobile carrier's standard rates apply.

Opt out and help

You can cancel at any time by replying STOP to any message. After you send STOP, we will send one confirmation message and then stop sending you texts. For help, reply HELP, email richellestowe.res@gmail.com, or visit our website.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Your privacy matters to us. See our Privacy Policy. We do not sell or share your mobile number or messaging consent with third parties for their own marketing.
